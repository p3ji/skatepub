How to upload to Google Calendar:
  1. Click the download button above to save the .ics file to your computer.
  2. Open Google Calendar in your web browser.
  3. In the top right, click the Settings gear icon > Settings.
  4. On the left menu, click Import & export.
  5. Click Select file from your computer and choose the downloaded .ics file for your program.
  6. Choose which calendar you want to add the events to, then click Import.
